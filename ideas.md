Here are 20+ mini-game concepts that would fit in our Space Math Academy:

## Action/Movement Games
1. **Hyperdrive Gates** - Your spaceship idea: fly through gates marked with correct answers, avoid wrong ones. Add barrel rolls and speed boosts.

2. **Asteroid Belt Navigation** - Side-scrolling where you must shoot asteroids displaying incorrect answers while preserving those with correct ones.

3. **Planet Hopping** - Jump between planets in correct numerical sequence, with gravity affecting your trajectory based on planet size.

4. **Solar Wind Surfing** - Ride solar wind currents by adjusting your "math sail" angle to catch numbers that sum to target values.

5. **Wormhole Calculator** - Enter correct coordinates to jump through wormholes, wrong answers send you to random dangerous locations.

## Strategy/Building Games
6. **Space Station Constructor** - Build orbital stations by placing modules that connect only when math problems match.

7. **Satellite Defense Grid** - Tower defense where defensive satellites only fire when you solve their targeting equations correctly.

8. **Resource Mining Operation** - Extract materials from asteroids based on calculating optimal extraction rates and storage capacity.

9. **Alien Trade Negotiations** - Barter with different alien species using their number systems and exchange rates.

10. **Colony Management** - Manage oxygen, food, and power on Mars colony using resource calculation puzzles.

## Logic/Pattern Games
11. **Constellation Code Breaker** - Connect stars to form constellations, with each connection requiring solving sequential math problems.

12. **Alien Message Decoder** - Decrypt alien transmissions by recognizing mathematical patterns and number sequences.

13. **Gravity Well Puzzles** - Use gravitational fields to move objects along paths, calculating trajectory angles and velocities.

14. **Time Dilation Challenges** - Solve problems involving different time rates on various planets and near black holes.

15. **Orbital Mechanics Simulator** - Calculate launch windows, fuel requirements, and orbital transfers between planets.

## Collection/Sorting Games
16. **Comet Harvest** - Catch comets in numerical order as they streak across the sky, with increasing speeds per grade level.

17. **Space Archaeology** - Piece together alien artifacts by solving geometric and numerical relationships.

18. **Stellar Classification** - Sort stars by brightness, size, temperature using magnitude calculations.

19. **Cargo Bay Tetris** - Load cargo ships with containers that must be arranged to match weight and volume calculations.

20. **Galaxy Census** - Count and categorize different space objects, practicing skip counting and grouping.

## Advanced Concepts
21. **Black Hole Escape** - Navigate around event horizons by calculating escape velocities and safe distances.

22. **Terraforming Equations** - Transform planets by adjusting atmospheric composition using chemical equations.

23. **Space Weather Prediction** - Forecast solar storms and cosmic radiation using pattern recognition and data analysis.
