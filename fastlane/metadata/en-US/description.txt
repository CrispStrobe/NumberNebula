Blast off into a galaxy of math! Numbernauts turns arithmetic, logic, and spatial reasoning into 20+ hand-crafted, space-themed mini-games for primary-school explorers and up.

Every mission is a different kind of puzzle, so practice never feels like a worksheet:

• Cosmic Triangles & Magic Squares — balance numbers so every side and line matches.
• Arithmancer Crosswords — fill a grid so all the intersecting equations work out.
• Komm-Relais — crack a Caesar cipher with a real cipher wheel to decode alien signals.
• Codebreaker & Cryptex Lock Breaker — solve interlocking equations to reveal the secret.
• Cargo Loader — a push-optimal Sokoban for cosmic logistics.
• Asteroid Hunter, Hyperdrive Gates & Gravity Sling — fast, arcade-style number challenges.
• 3D Block Counter & Anomaly Scan — rotate, count, and match to train spatial thinking.
• KenKen, Number Walls, Signal Triangulation, and many more.

Built for young learners:
• Adaptive difficulty that quietly focuses on the facts your child finds tricky.
• Grades 1–4 progression across number ranges and operations (+, −, ×, ÷).
• English and German throughout.
• Fully offline. No ads. No accounts. No data collection. No third-party tracking.

Whether it's a five-minute warm-up or a longer study session, Numbernauts makes numbers an adventure.
