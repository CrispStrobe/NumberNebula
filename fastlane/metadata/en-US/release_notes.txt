Six more mini-games, and every one of them played through to a win.

- Star Forge: the walkthrough explains correctly what an "arm" is, and the moves counter says "moves left" instead of turning red on every correct move
- Cube Scanner: a wrong answer shows the working ("Opposite faces add up to 7: 7 − 5 = 2"), and the button says "Check answer" instead of announcing a win in advance
- Relic Assembly: bigger pieces and edge numbers on tablets, and a larger rotate button
- Galactic Market's confirm button, the flashcard boxes, Hull Plating's rotate button and the crash-report count were still in English
- Walkthrough cards fit a phone held sideways, and greyed-out buttons are visible on the dark background
- Cargo-Loader could, rarely, deal a newly generated level that could not be won; fixed
