# KOMPASS mathematics: construction, planning and reasoning

This extension builds on the complete optional mathematics pathway. Its purpose
is to give learners more opportunities to construct a solution, plan several
steps, explain a relationship and disprove a claim. The existing free-play games
remain useful enrichment. These new activities share the course's saved drafts,
staged hints and first-response records.

Work is checkpointed on `codex/kompass4-reasoning-expansion-20261109`, based on
`8971225`. App version: **1.6.0+66**. The original four labs keep their identifiers,
seed algorithms and variant cycles, so an existing draft still means the same
mathematical activity after updating.

## Delivery plan

| New lab | Learner actions | Project competencies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reasoning workshop | Solve an additive wall backwards; optimize its base and explain the middle value; follow a rule machine; construct all permitted codes. | M-Z04, M-Z08, M-Z09, M-Z12 |
| Journey planner | Plan backwards from an arrival deadline; read services and allow for walking or connection time. | M-G05 |
| Area studio | Count unit squares and boundary edges; construct different connected, hole-free shapes satisfying area and perimeter constraints. | M-R02, M-R03, M-R08 |
| Chance detective | Construct a legal counterexample to a claim about two draws, respecting whether tokens are replaced. | M-D04 |

The written bank gains 16 original bilingual tasks: eight practice, four
independent-check and four rehearsal items. These add number-grid rules, grid-path
enumeration, multi-step stock stories, overlapping areas, simultaneous building
views, deposits and refunds, partial pouring, fixed-charge offers, overlapping
time intervals, tallies, majorities, chart-scale comparisons and games with neutral
outcomes. The existing 20-item printable rehearsal remains stable.

## Content and evidence rules

All new words, values, diagrams and scoring rules are authored for NumberNebula.
The competency mapping and reviewed mathematical formats inform the design;
official papers and private task prose are not embedded. The official scope
includes mathematical processes as well as the four content domains; see the
[IBBW conception](https://ibbw-bw.de/,Lde/Startseite/Pruefungen%2B_%2BLernstandserhebungen/Kompass%2B4%2B_%2BKonzeption).

Labs remain practice evidence, including when a learner solves them without a
hint. Partial points distinguish individual steps and a valid construction from
its explanation. The finite course bank's family labels describe authored task
families; they do not prove complete conceptual novelty or calibrated transfer.

Content version **2.1.0** will retain every original task unchanged. A build-time
comparison against the immutable 2.0.1 baseline will explicitly identify compatible
task IDs. Only those verified identical tasks may resume an old session or use an
old record's rubric. Changed or unknown historical content must still fail closed.

## Validation gates

- [ ] Independent mathematical checks for all new generator families.
- [ ] New controls, partial attempts, hints, restoration and compact layouts.
- [ ] All 16 written tasks independently checked; generated content and print
  assets reproducible.
- [ ] Original recipes and existing task identities unchanged.
- [ ] Version compatibility checked against the pinned baseline.
- [ ] Exact-head remote analysis, full tests, calibration and browser checks.

Checks and run links are recorded after execution. This extension does not claim
an official score, a school recommendation or measured learning gains.
